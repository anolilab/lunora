---
title: Serving your app's responses
description: The headers Lunora Cloud adds to an app's responses, what a browser does with them, and which deployments get them.
---

Apps served on a `*.lunora.app` address or a custom domain get
`X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` on every response, so a browser never guesses a
type. Apps deployed to your own Cloudflare account are plain Workers and do not go
through Lunora Cloud, so they do not get these headers.

A script or stylesheet served with the wrong `Content-Type` is blocked, not run.
Check that your app sends `text/javascript` for JavaScript and `text/css` for CSS.

The platform's `Referrer-Policy` default, `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, applies
unless your app sets its own.

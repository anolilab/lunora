Every Lunora app ships with **Studio** — a local admin UI for your schema, data, SQL, logs, and
time-travel, running against your live edge database. No extra setup, no separate dashboard to host.

## See your data as it changes

Studio subscribes to the same live queries your app does, so the data view updates in realtime as
mutations land. It is the fastest way to understand what your backend is actually doing.

## Rewind any shard

Because every shard is a SQLite database with point-in-time history, Studio lets you rewind to any
moment in the last 30 days and inspect — or restore — past state.

## Advisors built in

Schema and query lints surface unindexed foreign keys, duplicate indexes, and other footguns before
they reach production, right inside the Advisors table.

Studio is included with every project — run `lunora dev` and open it alongside your app.
